# Operating Rhythm (Cadence)

## Standing meetings
| Meeting | Frequency | Participants | Inputs | Outputs |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Program Core (HW/Controls/Facilities) | Weekly | Leads + TPM | IMS deltas, RAID, blockers | Updated plan, owners, due dates |
| Integration Readiness Review | Weekly | Eng + Test + Ops | readiness checklist | go/no-go decisions |
| Supplier Sync | Weekly/biweekly | SCM + Eng | open PO items, LLT | commitments, escalations |
| Exec Staff (1-pager) | Weekly | PM + Sponsor | weekly report | decisions, escalations |

## Artifacts (single source of truth)
- IMS: `data/.../ims_tasks.csv`
- RAID: `docs/raids/RAID_LOG.md`
- Decisions: `docs/raids/DECISION_LOG.md`
- Change control: `docs/raids/CHANGE_CONTROL.md`
- Weekly report: `docs/reports/WEEKLY_EXEC_UPDATE_TEMPLATE.md`
